# Water harvesting
Zai pits (planting pits about 20 to 30 cm wide and 10 to 15 cm deep filled with manure or compost) and half-moon micro-catchments concentrate runoff and nutrients around the plant and improve establishment on crusted soils.

# Micro-dosing
Placing a small dose of fertilizer (a bottle cap, about 2 to 6 grams) in each planting hole at sowing or shortly after emergence increases yields of millet and sorghum at low cost compared with broadcasting.

# Striga (witchweed)
Striga is a parasitic weed that attaches to cereal roots. Rotate or intercrop cereals with legumes such as cowpea or groundnut, use Striga-tolerant varieties where available, add organic matter and fertilizer, and uproot Striga plants before they set seed.

# Drought risk
Early-maturing varieties reduce exposure to end-of-season drought. If rains start late, prefer shorter-cycle varieties and do not plant long-cycle varieties after the recommended latest sowing date.
