# Rice blast
Blast causes diamond-shaped lesions with grey centres on leaves and can rot the neck of the panicle. It is favoured by high humidity, long leaf wetness and excess nitrogen. Use resistant varieties, clean seed, balanced nitrogen applied in splits, and remove infected stubble.

# Water management
Level fields so that water depth is uniform. Alternate wetting and drying (AWD), letting the water drop about 15 cm below the soil surface before re-flooding, can save water without yield loss once plants are established, but keep fields flooded around flowering.
