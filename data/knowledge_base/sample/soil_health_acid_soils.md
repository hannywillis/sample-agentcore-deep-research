# Soil testing
Test soil every two to three years. A composite sample from 10 to 15 spots taken at 0 to 20 cm depth gives a reliable picture of pH, organic carbon and nutrients.

# Acid soils
Most cereals grow best at a soil pH between about 5.5 and 7.0. Below pH 5.5 phosphorus becomes less available and aluminium toxicity can damage roots. Agricultural lime applied a few weeks before planting and incorporated into the soil raises pH; the rate depends on the soil test.

# Organic matter
Retaining crop residues, adding compost or manure and rotating with legumes increase soil organic carbon, improving structure, water holding and nutrient supply.
