# About this document
Illustrative sample for pipeline testing. Recommendations differ by agro-ecological zone and variety; replace with validated KALRO recommendations.

# Planting time
Plant at the onset of the long rains once the soil is moist to at least 30 cm depth, usually after 20 to 30 mm of cumulative rainfall in a few days. Late planting by two or more weeks commonly reduces yield and increases pest pressure.

# Variety choice
Choose certified seed of a variety adapted to the zone: early-maturing, drought-tolerant varieties for lowland and dry mid-altitude areas, and medium to late maturing hybrids for high-potential highlands with reliable rainfall.

# Spacing
A common spacing is 75 cm between rows and 25 to 30 cm between plants with one seed per hole, giving roughly 44,000 to 53,000 plants per hectare. In drier areas wider spacing lowers competition for moisture.

# Fertilizer
Apply a phosphorus-containing fertilizer such as DAP or NPK at planting, placed in the planting hole and mixed with soil so that seed does not touch the fertilizer. Top-dress with a nitrogen fertilizer such as CAN when plants are knee high, about 4 to 6 weeks after emergence, when the soil is moist. Base rates on a soil test where possible. Well-decomposed manure improves soil organic matter and water holding capacity.

# Weeding
Keep the field weed-free during the first 6 to 8 weeks; the first weeding is usually 2 to 3 weeks after emergence.
