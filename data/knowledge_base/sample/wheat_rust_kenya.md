# Symptoms
Stem rust produces elongated, reddish-brown pustules on stems and leaf sheaths. Yellow (stripe) rust produces yellow pustules arranged in stripes along the leaves and is favoured by cool, wet conditions in the highlands.

# Management
Grow rust-resistant varieties recommended for the area and replace varieties whose resistance has broken down. Avoid very late planting, which exposes the crop to higher spore loads. Scout regularly from tillering. Where a registered fungicide is needed, apply at early disease onset following the label; consult an extension officer for product choice and timing.
