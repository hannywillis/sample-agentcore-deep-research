# Why it matters
Aflatoxins are poisonous substances produced by Aspergillus moulds. Contaminated grain is dangerous for people and livestock and cannot be made safe by cooking.

# Harvest and drying
Harvest promptly at maturity, avoid leaving maize on wet ground, and dry grain on tarpaulins or raised platforms, not bare soil. Dry to 13 percent moisture or lower before storage; a simple salt-in-a-jar test can indicate whether grain is dry enough.

# Storage
Sort out damaged, discoloured and mouldy cobs. Store in clean, dry hermetic bags or metal silos on pallets off the floor. Do not eat or sell visibly mouldy grain; seek advice from an extension officer or public health officer if contamination is suspected.
